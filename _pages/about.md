Hi, I am a Senior Postdoctoral Associate in the Department of Mechanical Engineering at [Boston University](https://www.bu.edu/), working in the [Skeletal Mechanobiology and Biomechanics Laboratory](https://morganresearchlab.org/) with Professor [Elise F. Morgan](https://www.bu.edu/eng/profile/elise-morgan-ph-d/). Previously, I completed my Ph.D. in the Department of Civil and Systems Engineering at [Johns Hopkins University](https://www.jhu.edu/), advised by Professor [Stavros Gaitanaros](https://orbit.dtu.dk/en/persons/stavros-gaitanaros/). Before that, I earned my M.S. in Civil Engineering from [Northwestern University](https://www.northwestern.edu/) and my B.S. in Civil Engineering from [Zhejiang University](https://www.zju.edu.cn/english/).

Research Interests
======
My research lies at the intersection of mechanics, materials, biology, and artificial intelligence within the broad realm of architected materials, spanning engineered metamaterials, human bone, and biomedical scaffolds, to deliver fundamental scientific insights and develop innovative material solutions to address pressing societal challenges through the following research thrusts:
- Mechanics, design, and manufacturing of engineered metamaterials
- Data-driven multiscale mechanics of human bone
- Generative AI for discovery and design of multifunctional architected materials
